Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before applying? Here are answers to common queries from parents and learners.

Is this program fully online or offline?

We offer a flexible hybrid model: digital live/recorded classes combined with local mentor guidance and parent check-ins.

What are the course fees?

Fees are kept minimal and tailored to rural and semi-urban families. Specific batch pricing is provided during counselling.

Will students get digital access and study material?

Yes, enrolled students receive access through their registered mobile number with downloadable notes and practice tests.
