Academic Framework

Structured 3-Tier Mentorship Framework

Every student is supported by three distinct specialists ensuring academic performance, personal growth, and prompt issue resolution.

👨‍🏫

Tier 1: Lead Teachers

Senior subject experts delivering core conceptual masterclasses, board-aligned curriculum, and interactive problem-solving techniques.

👩‍💼

Tier 2: Personal Mentors

Dedicated advisors monitoring batch attendance, assignment submissions, parent syncs, and individual academic goal setting.

🙋‍♂️

Tier 3: Academic Guides
